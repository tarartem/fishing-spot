## 1. Domain Glossary

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Angler** | A user of the application (`guest` or `registered` via Email auth). |
| **Water Body Pin (Level 1 Pin)** | A map marker with a label placed over a water body (lake, river, reservoir/*embalse*, or coastal fishing zone). **Visible to all users (including guests) on app load.** Clicking it opens the water body's basic description, catchable fish species, sample feedback, and CTA. |
| **Tactical Sector** | For small lakes (`< 5km` diameter), the entire water body polygon; for large reservoirs, rivers, or sea coastlines, a clipped `~2–3km` radius / viewport sector around the water body pin or clicked point. |
| **Spatial Grid** | A 2D coordinate matrix of labeled cells (`A1` through `H8`, each `100m–300m` wide) generated deterministically by `Turf.js` over the Tactical Sector. **Unlocked for Registered Users.** |
| **Grid Cell** | A single square cell of the Spatial Grid carrying deterministic metadata: center `[lat, lng]`, polygon bounds, water/shoreline intersection, OSM structural tags, and restriction status. |
| **Grid Fishing Spot Pin (Level 2 Pin)** | An exact fishing location inside a Grid Cell generated by the AI Advisor or pre-seeded on the grid, containing tactical descriptions of where and how to catch fish. **Restricted to Registered Users.** |
| **Review / Catch Log** | Angler feedback, star rating (1–5), fish caught, and client-compressed WebP catch photos attached to a Water Body Pin (with optional grid spot reference). |

## 2. Taxonomy & Classification Rules

### 2.1 Water Types (`WaterType` — Core Input #1)
- `freshwater`: Lakes, ponds, reservoirs (*embalses*), rivers, streams, canals.
- `sea`: Coastal beaches, rocky shores, cliffs, piers, jetties, harbors, estuaries.

### 2.2 Fishing Techniques (`FishingType` — Core Input #2)
- `spinning`: Lure fishing / predator angling.
- `feeder_bottom`: Feeder, method feeder, ledgering, bottom rigs.
- `carp`: Specialist carp angling (boilies, hair rigs).
- `float`: Match, waggler, pole, bolognese, or bobber fishing.
- `fly`: Fly fishing (dry fly, nymphing, streamers).
- `surfcasting`: Beach / coastal long-distance shore casting.

### 2.3 Target Fish Species (`TargetSpecies` — Core Input #3, Focus: Poland & Spain)
- **Freshwater Predators**: Northern Pike (`pike`), Zander (`zander`), European Perch (`perch`), Black Bass (`black_bass`), Trout (`trout`), Wels Catfish (`catfish`), Asp (`asp`), Chub (`chub`), Grayling (`grayling`).
- **Freshwater Coarse & Carp**: Common/Mirror Carp (`carp`), Common Bream (`bream`), Tench (`tench`), Roach (`roach`), Barbel (`barbel`), Crucian Carp (`crucian_carp`).
- **Sea & Coastal**: European Sea Bass (`sea_bass`), Gilthead Seabream (`dorada`), Atlantic Mackerel (`mackerel`), White Seabream (`sargo`), Bluefish (`bluefish`), Garfish (`garfish`), Sea Trout (`sea_trout`), European Flounder (`flounder`), Atlantic Cod (`cod`), Grey Mullet (`mullet`).
- **Wildcard**: `any`.

### 2.4 Access Mode (`AccessMode` — Core Input #4)
- `shore`: Fishing from the bank, beach, rocky shore, pier, or wading.
- `boat`: Fishing from a boat, kayak, or belly boat.

### 2.5 Restriction Status (`RestrictionStatus`)
- `allowed` | `permit_required` | `seasonal_restriction` | `prohibited` | `unknown`

## 3. Core Business Invariants
1. **Two-Tier Map & Content Visibility (Guest vs. Registered)**:
   - **Guests**:
     - See the map with **Water Body Pins and their names**.
     - Clicking a Water Body Pin displays the **basic description** (overview, what fish species can be caught there, teaser community feedback, and a Call to Action to register).
     - Guests can trigger up to 2 AI basic water-body overview analyses if exploring a water body not yet summarized, returning only the basic water-body level info (species + overview).
     - **Guests NEVER see the Spatial Grid (`A1..H8`) or exact Fishing Spot Pins/coordinates**; unlocking the grid and all full content requires Email registration.
   - **Registered Users**:
     - Unlock the **Spatial Grid**, **exact Fishing Spot Pins** on the grid, full tactical descriptions (baits/lures/presentation), single-cell (`D5`) deep-dives, and full community reviews/photos.
2. **No Prohibited Spot Recommendations**:
   - Any grid cell intersecting a `prohibited` zone is deterministically flagged and never recommended as a fishable spot.
3. **Water-Bound & Shore-Consistent Coordinates**:
   - Every Grid Fishing Spot Pin `[lat, lng]` must reside inside a valid water/shoreline grid cell.
