## 1. Domain Glossary

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Angler** | A user of the application searching for fishing locations or contributing reviews/catches. |
| **Water Body** | A distinct hydrological feature selected on the map (lake, pond, river segment, canal, reservoir, sea bay, or coastal stretch). |
| **Spatial Grid** | A 2D matrix of numbered cells (e.g., `A1` through `H8`) generated over the bounding box/polygon of a selected Water Body. |
| **Grid Cell** | A single square/hexagonal sector of the Spatial Grid containing metadata: center coordinates, water/shoreline coverage, known map features, and restriction status. |
| **Suggested Spot** | A specific recommended location inside a Grid Cell generated by the AI Assistant, complete with coordinates, tactical description, recommended baits/lures, and confidence score. |
| **Restriction Zone** | A geographic polygon or rule indicating whether fishing is prohibited, seasonal, permit-only, or catch-and-release only. |
| **Spot Review / Catch Log** | User-generated feedback on a Spot, including a star rating, text notes, fish caught, and uploaded photos. |

## 2. Taxonomy & Classification Rules

### 2.1 Water Types (`WaterType`)
- `freshwater_still`: Lakes, ponds, reservoirs, gravel pits, quarries.
- `freshwater_flowing`: Rivers, streams, creeks, canals.
- `brackish`: Estuaries, river mouths, coastal lagoons.
- `saltwater_coastal`: Sea beaches, rocky shores, piers, jetties, harbors.
- `saltwater_offshore`: Deeper sea zones requiring a boat/kayak.

### 2.2 Fishing Styles / Techniques (`FishingType`)
- `spinning` (Lure fishing / predator angling)
- `feeder_bottom` (Feeder, ledgering, carp angling)
- `float` (Match / pole / bobber fishing)
- `fly` (Fly fishing — dry fly, nymph, streamer)
- `surfcasting` (Beach / shore sea casting)
- `trolling_boat` (Boat / kayak trolling or vertical jigging)
- `ice` (Winter ice fishing)

### 2.3 Target Fish Species Taxonomy (European Focus)
- **European Freshwater Predators**:
  - Northern Pike (`esox_lucius`), Zander / Pike-perch (`sander_lucioperca`), European Perch (`perca_fluviatilis`), Brown/Rainbow Trout (`salmo_trutta` / `oncorhynchus_mykiss`), Wels Catfish (`silurus_glanis`), Asp (`leuciscus_aspius`), Chub (`squalius_cephalus`), Grayling (`thymallus_thymallus`).
- **European Freshwater Coarse / Carp**:
  - Common/Mirror Carp (`cyprinus_carpio`), Common Bream (`abramis_brama`), Tench (`tinca_tinca`), Roach (`rutilus_rutilus`), Barbel (`barbus_barbus`), Crucian Carp (`carassius_carassius`).
- **European Sea & Coastal**:
  - European Sea Bass (`dicentrarchus_labrax`), Gilthead Seabream (`sparus_aurata`), Atlantic Mackerel (`scomber_scombrus`), Atlantic Cod (`gadus_morhua`), Sea Trout (`salmo_trutta_trutta`), Garfish (`belone_belone`), European Flounder (`platichthys_flesus`), Grey Mullet (`chelon_labrosus`).

### 2.4 Access Mode (`AccessMode`)
- `shore`: Fishing from the bank, beach, pier, or wading.
- `boat`: Fishing from a boat, kayak, or belly boat.

### 2.4 Restriction Status (`RestrictionStatus`)
- `allowed`: Open for recreational fishing (standard license may still apply).
- `permit_required`: Private fishery, club water, or special day-ticket required.
- `seasonal_restriction`: Closed during spawning seasons or specific months.
- `prohibited`: Strictly forbidden (nature reserve, dam safety zone, sanctuaries, military/private port).
- `unknown`: No verified regulatory data found; user must check local signage.

## 3. Core Business Invariants
1. **No Prohibited Spot Recommendations**: The AI Advisor must never output a `prohibited` cell as a recommended fishing spot.
2. **Water-Bound Coordinates**: Every suggested spot pin `[lat, lng]` must lie inside or within 30 meters of the selected water body polygon.
3. **Shore vs. Boat Consistency**: When `AccessMode == shore`, suggested grid cells must intersect the shoreline, a pier/bridge, or be within casting distance (<= 80m) of accessible bank.
4. **Review Authenticity**: Reviews and catch photos must be linked to a specific `fishing_spot` (or grid cell) and an authenticated `user`.
